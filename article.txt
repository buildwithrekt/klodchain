Vibecoded projects are rewriting the rules of who gets to build

Something shifted in 2024. Projects started appearing that shouldn't exist. Not because the ideas were impossible, but because the teams behind them were too small. One person shipping what used to require ten. A weekend prototype matching the output of a three-month sprint.

The term is vibecoding. You describe what you want in natural language, an AI writes the code, you iterate until it works. No planning documents. No sprint ceremonies. No waiting for the senior dev to review your PR. Just building.

The crypto space noticed first. Probably because crypto Twitter rewards speed over polish, and because the barrier between "idea" and "token" has always been uncomfortably thin. But what's happening now goes beyond memecoin generators and trading bots.

Infrastructure is getting vibecoded

The interesting projects aren't wrappers anymore. They're actual infrastructure. Blockchain explorers. DEX interfaces. Entire L2 concepts prototyped in days.

klodchain is one example: a full Solana-style blockchain with AI validators, bonding curves, and token economics. Built by one person using Claude Code. The agents produce blocks. The AMM calculates prices. Users trade tokens. It runs.

A year ago this would have required a funded startup with backend engineers, smart contract devs, and frontend specialists. Now it requires one person who understands what they want to build and can articulate it clearly.

The skill that matters changed. Deep knowledge of a specific framework matters less than the ability to architect systems and communicate intent. The AI handles implementation details. The human handles vision and taste.

Why blockchain benefits disproportionately

Blockchain has always had a builder shortage. Too much demand for smart contract auditors, protocol engineers, and infrastructure devs. Not enough supply. Projects waited months for talent that never came.

Vibecoding compresses that timeline. A founder with domain expertise but no Solidity knowledge can now prototype their DeFi mechanism. A researcher with a novel consensus idea can build a working simulation. The bottleneck shifts from "can we find someone to build this" to "is this worth building."

The implications for decentralization are significant. When building is expensive, only funded teams ship. When building is cheap, anyone with conviction ships. The distribution of who creates protocols widens.

Crypto was supposed to democratize finance. Vibecoding might actually democratize crypto development itself.

The quality question

Critics argue vibecoded projects lack rigor. The code isn't optimized. Edge cases aren't handled. Security assumptions go untested.

They're right. Most vibecoded projects shouldn't go to mainnet without serious review. A bonding curve that works in a POC might have exploit vectors that only surface under adversarial conditions.

But this misses the point. Vibecoding excels at the 0-to-1 phase. Getting something that works, that demonstrates a concept, that proves or disproves a hypothesis. The 1-to-production phase still needs traditional engineering discipline.

The projects that understand this distinction will thrive. Vibecode the prototype, then bring in specialists to harden it. Move fast to validate, slow down to secure.

What changes when execution cost approaches zero

The market for ideas restructures. When execution was expensive, good ideas with weak teams died. Funding decisions weighted team credentials heavily because shipping was hard.

Now execution is cheap. A strong idea with a single competent builder has a path to a working product. The weight shifts toward the idea itself, toward taste, toward understanding what users actually want.

This rewards domain experts who couldn't previously code over generalist engineers who could code but lacked domain insight. The person who deeply understands MEV but never learned Rust can now build MEV tools. The DeFi researcher who only wrote papers can now ship protocols.

The gatekeepers of "technical enough to build" lose their gates.

The vibecoded future

In five years, most crypto projects will start as vibecoded prototypes. The ones that find traction will attract traditional engineering talent for the hardening phase. The distinction between "technical" and "non-technical" founders will blur until it becomes meaningless.

Blockchain's next wave of innovation won't come from established teams with deep benches. It will come from individuals who see problems clearly and can now, finally, build solutions themselves.

The tools exist. The paradigm shifted. The only question left is what you're going to build.
